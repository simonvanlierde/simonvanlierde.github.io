# Simon van Lierde's work

This site helps industrial ecology researchers, research software users and collaborators understand the software and data I build.

Readers can inspect a working project, find its source and release metadata, check merged contributions, and read or download the CV. Project titles open the website when one exists; source links remain separate.

The index introduces R9lab and four featured projects: jsonvet, credit-matrix, ramascene-explorer and cv-ie-taxonomy. It also presents five upstream libraries. The archive holds the remaining projects and the complete upstream contribution list. Featured means selected for this introduction. Archive means outside that selection, not discontinued.

The project register uses the recorded item, kind, stack and links. It omits year, role and maintenance status because the project cards do not record those facts. Release versions, DOIs and licences come from the cached CodeMeta records rather than a second manual list.

The CV keeps its dated experience history and exported PDF. The index's R9lab totals are a dated snapshot of the live platform; historical totals in the CV retain the date of their original claim.
